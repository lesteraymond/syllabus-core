Benefits of the system

Streamlined curriculum monitoringDashboards show submissions, deadlines, and compliance for each program.
Standardized syllabus reviewOne ten-section LCUP template and one ordered review for every syllabus.
Less manual checkingCoded rules and AI point out gaps before a reviewer opens the syllabus.
Feedback in one placeComments and review progress stay with the syllabus.
Protected recordsApproved syllabi keep their version history and review trail.
