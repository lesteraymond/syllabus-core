System overview

The AI-Powered Web-Based Syllabus Management System is one platform for preparing, checking, reviewing, approving, and storing course syllabi in the CITE Department of La Consolacion University Philippines.

Problems it solves

Inconsistent formats and duplicate files
Alignment checked only by eye
Comments scattered in email and chat
No single record of who reviewed which version

Objectives

To standardize how course syllabi are prepared, checked, reviewed, approved, and stored.

01Build syllabi that follow LCUP's ten-section template through a guided builder, PDF import, and AI drafting.
02Check each syllabus against coded rules and constructive alignment, and propose revisions for people to decide on.
03Route each syllabus through the Dean, Asst. VPAA, and LIC Director before the VPAA decides.
04Support monitoring with dashboards, deadlines, reports, a versioned repository, and an audit log.

Key features and functionalities

Syllabus Builder

Seven guided steps with official course data and autosave.

PDF Import

Scans an existing syllabus and checks it against the template.

AI Syllabus Generator

Drafts outcomes, the 18-week plan, and grading for a subject.

AI Checker

Judges alignment, scores it, and suggests revisions. AI advises; it never approves.

Compliance Check

Blocks missing sections and grading totals other than 100%.

Review & Approval

Four reviewers in order. A returned syllabus goes back to whoever returned it.

Repository

Approved syllabi with version history.

Analytics & Reports

Trends with CSV and DOCX export.

Curriculum and Users & Roles

Programs, subjects, and deadlines, with access for six roles.
