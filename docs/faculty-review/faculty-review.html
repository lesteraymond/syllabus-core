Course description (from the sample syllabus)

This course introduces students to the vital role of computers and computer programmers in shaping modern society. It
aims to foster a strong foundation in logical formulation techniques essential for programming, while promoting the
development of good coding practices. Students will be taught the fundamentals of Java programming and explore
significant real-world applications.
In line with La Consolacion University Philippines' commitment to forming Christo-centric Catholic professionals , the
course integrates Marian Augustinian values in each lesson, ensuring that students not only achieve intellectual growth
but also cultivate moral and ethical responsibility in the digital age. This holistic approach nurtures individuals who contribute
positively to society, embodying community, humility, and love of service , as exemplified by the Blessed Virgin Mary
and Saint Augustine. By the end of the course, students will have created their own Java applications, demonstrating both
technical proficiency and a sense of purpose rooted in their Catholic faith.

maximize performance, minimize waste, and support organizational goals.

This course covers operational amplifiers, signal converters, power switching devices and the construction and operation

students to design technology that promotes social responsibility, collaboration, and compassion. Through hands-on
projects, learners will not only develop technical proficiency but also embody ethical stewardship — using their knowledge to
create solutions that serve the needs of others and contribute to the common good.
