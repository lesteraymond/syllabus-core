II.   COURSE TITLE   :   Systems Engineering 
III.   COURSE DESCRIPTION   : 
This course introduces students to the principles and practices of systems engineering, focusing on the analysis,
design, and management of complex systems. Through a blend of theoretical concepts and practical applications, students
learn   how   to   approach   and   solve   large-scale   engineering   problems   involving   multiple   components,   stakeholders,   and
operational constraints. Key topics include requirements analysis, systems design, integration, project management, and
performance evaluation. 
Students will develop skills in modeling and optimizing systems that integrate people, processes, and technology. They
will explore methods for ensuring that systems are efficient, reliable, and adaptable across various industries, such as
manufacturing, healthcare, and logistics. This course prepares BSIE students to play a vital role in creating systems that
maximize performance, minimize waste, and support organizational goals. 
Beyond   technical   knowledge,   the   course   integrates   Marian   Augustinian   values   into   each   lesson,   fostering   the
development of students as Christo-centric Catholic professionals. The curriculum not only aims for intellectual growth but also
instills a commitment to ethical decision-making, social responsibility, and compassionate service. Students will be equipped
with the knowledge and values necessary to thrive as professionals in the field of Industrial Engineering.
