# Engineering Program Outcomes: source comparison

Local-only comparison, 2026-10-28. No official outcomes or database records were changed. Page numbers are PDF page numbers (1-based). Text below normalizes whitespace only; exact line breaks, source labels, pages and SHA-256 values are in `engineering-pos.json` and the source evidence.

IE-403 and CPE-404 are supporting PO references only. Their CLOs, plans and other academic content are not baseline checker inputs.

Both supporting documents print PO12 in full on page 3:

> Knowledge and understanding of engineering and management principles as a member and leader in a team, to manage projects and in multidisciplinary environments.

The baseline IE-402 (p3) and CpE304 (p3) stop after “and in”. The completion is evidence for an institutional correction, not permission to alter official records or baseline text.

Beyond PO12, IE-402 vs IE-403 differs at PO2, PO4, PO5, PO7, PO10 and PO11. CpE304 vs CPE-404 differs at PO4 and PO7. Baseline IE-402 vs CpE304 differs only at PO11 (“and;” / “and”). The two supporting documents differ at PO2, PO5, PO7 and PO10 (period / semicolon). All remaining lexical wording is the same; these are not silent spelling corrections.

The sources do not establish which punctuation is official, whether engineering programs share one formal list, whether the identical PO13 belongs to BSCpE, or a PO–EGA crosswalk. These decisions remain with VPAA/Dean (L2/L3). PO12 is no longer missing from all available sources, but its official adoption/version is still pending.
