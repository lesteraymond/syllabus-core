II.   COURSE TITLE   :   Operation Management 
III.   COURSE DESCRIPTION   : 
This course provides students with a comprehensive understanding of the principles, strategies, and tools used in
managing operations within manufacturing and service organizations. It explores key topics such as operations strategy,
process design, capacity planning, supply chain management, inventory control, quality assurance, and project scheduling.
Emphasis is placed on applying quantitative and qualitative methods to solve real-world operational challenges, improve
productivity, and enhance organizational competitiveness. Through case studies, simulations, and team projects, students
will develop critical thinking and decision-making skills essential for effective operations leadership in dynamic business
environments. 
Anchored in Marian and Augustinian values, this course introduces students to the principles and tools of managing
operations in manufacturing and service organizations. It emphasizes strategic thinking, process design, and data-driven
decision-making to solve real-world challenges and improve productivity in ethical and socially responsive ways.
