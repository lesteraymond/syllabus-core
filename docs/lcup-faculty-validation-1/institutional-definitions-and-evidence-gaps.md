# Unavailable institutional definitions and missing evaluation evidence

Package lcup-faculty-validation-1, prepared 2026-10-29. Written by hand for the research team. Do not give this document to reviewers before they have returned their forms: Part B names open questions about particular syllabus entries.

For each gap, this document records:
- what is missing;
- why it matters for the validation;
- who the existing records suggest should supply it;
- what the package does meanwhile.

Nothing here supplies a definition or a decision. Where the records name no owner, it says "not yet assigned".

The references point to:
- `docs/lcup-academic-decisions-for-vpaa.md`: Decisions 1 to 14, the course-faculty questions F1 to F10 and the L items;
- `docs/faculty-review/align-1-dev-adjudication-v2/coordinator-restricted/distributed-text/3-policy-panel/02-institutional-decisions-and-approvals.md`: the I items.

None of the items below is answered or recorded. The response record of the decisions document is empty, and so is the decision and approval record of the align-1 V2 package.

## B. Missing evaluation evidence

### B1. No faculty judgment exists

No genuine case of the benchmark has a faculty judgment: every one has an empty expected judgment and the review status "unassigned". The benchmark's labels for synthetic cases are not faculty-approved and are not used here. This package creates the means to collect judgments, not the judgments.

### B2. No Claude result exists for the sample

- No Claude output exists for any of the 21 cases. The three cases that already had one (the Policy V2 smoke) were left out.
- No approved run covers the sample. Of the prepared bundles:
  - `prepared-clo-classification-3` (prepared, not run) covers the 3 ILO ↔ CLO cases among its 65;
  - the other 18 cases have no prepared scope;
  - `prepared-final` and `prepared-r2` must be prepared again before any use.
- A comparison needs Decision 14b, a new pre-registered scope and the project owner's approval of the run and its cost (README, step 8).

### B3. Gaps in the sample's coverage

The sample uses only authentic syllabus statements. These are the gaps that could not be filled without inventing content:

| Relationship | Gap | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Course Title ↔ Course Content | No CpE 304 case, and no apparent misalignment | All 8 CpE 304 cases are reference-held (A4.2). No authentic apparent misalignment was found in the three syllabi. The third case is the IE-402 orientation week. |
| Course Content ↔ Learning Activities | No apparent misalignment | None found. The third case is a CpE 304 activity that names only a teaching method. |
| Course Content ↔ Assessment | No apparent partial alignment | An authentic candidate exists (reserve 1 in the selection record), but it would make a tenth IE-402 case, so the CpE 304 case was kept. |
| Learning Activities ↔ Assessment | No CpE 304 case | CpE 304 words its activities and assessments in general terms in every lesson, and FV-19 and FV-20 already represent that wording. |
| ILO ↔ CLO | No CLO 2 (Enabling) case | A candidate exists (reserve 2). IT 202 has no ILO mapped to CLO 4, and CpE 304 has none mapped to CLO 3 or CLO 4, so those classifications cannot be sampled in those courses. |

### B4. Relationships that cannot be evaluated

`coordinator-only/missing-mappings-and-non-evaluable.md` lists them one by one. In total, 26 relationships cannot be evaluated as they stand:
- 15 ILOs mapped to no CLO: 4 in IT 202 and 11 in CpE 304;
- 3 relationships of the CpE 304 Week 1 lesson, which has no ILO;
- 8 reference-held CpE 304 Course Title ↔ Course Content cases.

Separately, 3 CLOs are built by no ILO: IT 202 CLO 4, and CpE 304 CLO 3 and CLO 4. Of the 81 ILO ↔ CLO relationships, 65 can be evaluated, and the dataset lists all 81 separately, one per ILO and CLO.

### B5. Unanswered questions to the course faculty

The authors' answers to F1 to F10 are not available. Several touch the sample:
- F4 asks whether the Week 15 machine problem "Hands-on exercises using C programs" is intended (FV-05);
- F5 asks whether CLO 4 is meant to be tagged by ILO6 to ILO14 (FV-12);
- F6 notes that the label ILO10 is used for two different outcomes (FV-14);
- F2 asks which CLO the untagged ILOs build (FV-02 and FV-07 use untagged ILOs, as does FV-21, an F8 case).

Faculty judge the syllabi as printed and approved. An author's later explanation does not change the evidence, but the adjudicator may record it in the reason. Do not raise these questions with reviewers before they return their forms.

### B6. Development data, not a held-out test

The three syllabi are the only genuine syllabi available to the project, and they informed the design of the evaluation prompts. A Claude comparison on them is therefore a development-stage check. An independent accuracy estimate would need further approved syllabi, used with consent and kept apart from development. None is available.

### B7. Sample size

Three cases per relationship support counts and a qualitative review, not rates or significance tests. The sample was varied on purpose, so it says nothing about how often the syllabi are aligned. A larger sample would need more reviewer time and, for a held-out estimate, more syllabi (B6).

### B8. Transcription

The statements come from the project's transcription of the approved syllabi:
- line breaks were joined;
- spelling, numbering and tags were kept, including evident typing errors such as "aCLOng" and "Instrumentatio n".

Reviewers can report a suspected transcription error in the Notes column. The package does not correct the text.
