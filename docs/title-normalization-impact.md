Generated by `node scripts/compare-title-policy.cjs --write`. No provider and no database. **Exact** is the official comparison today (policy `shadow`). **Normalised** folds only Unicode form, letter case and whitespace (`server/lib/syllabuscheck/titles.js`); there is no fuzzy matching. **Nothing here is enforced.**

IT 202 is written without the parenthetical part of its approved title ("COMPUTER PROGRAMMING 1 (FUNDAMENTALS OF PROGRAMMING)"), so it stays failed under both policies, by design. IE 402 and CpE 304 differ only in capitalisation; under the normalised policy each loses one blocking finding (their other findings remain).

## 3. What does not change

- The points total (`provisionalScore`) and every deduction: the title check deducts no points.
- Course code, units, prerequisite, year level and curriculum year comparisons (unchanged code paths; tested).
- Reports already stored: after a policy switch, a report whose own title evidence would now be decided differently is shown as out of date (`titleDecisionChanged`), so no displayed result mixes the two policies.
