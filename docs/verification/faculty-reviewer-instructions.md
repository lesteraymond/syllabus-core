**Reviewer Guide & Operational Instructions**

## WELCOME & OVERVIEW

Thank you for serving as an expert faculty evaluator in the LCUP Syllabus Management System Academic Validation Pilot. Your professional judgment provides the essential foundation for verifying whether curriculum components in our outcome-based education (OBE) syllabi demonstrate genuine pedagogical alignment.

You are evaluating isolated **academic relationship pairs** from an approved institutional syllabus in your discipline:
- **BSIT Reviewers**: IT 202 (*Computer Programming 1*) — 7 Cases (`FV-01` to `FV-07`)
- **BSIE Reviewers**: IE 402 (*Systems Engineering*) — 9 Cases (`FV-08` to `FV-16`)
- **BSCpE Reviewers**: CPE 304 (*Fundamentals of Mixed Signals and Sensors*) — 5 Cases (`FV-17` to `FV-21`)

You will work independently using the supplied **Case Booklet** and **Evaluation Workbook**.

---

## 1. PREPARATION CHECKLIST

Before beginning your review, confirm that you have received the following reviewer package:
1. **Case Booklet** (`case-booklet.html`): Open this file in any web browser. It contains the course description, the Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) with their required classifications, the lesson schedule outline, and the full weekly context for your assigned cases.
2. **Academic Evaluation Rubric** (`academic-evaluation-rubric.md`): Review the definitions and relationship criteria.
3. **Your Assigned Evaluation Workbook** (`faculty-evaluation-form-[SubjectCode]-[ReviewerID].xlsx`): The Excel workbook where you will record your judgments.

---

## 2. CORE EVALUATION PRINCIPLES

1. **Independent Evaluation**: Complete your review alone. Do not discuss the cases with other faculty members, the syllabus authors, or outside reviewers until all evaluations are submitted.
2. **Strictly Human Evaluation**: Do **not** use AI tools, chatbots, or automated checkers. This study measures human expert consensus.
3. **Judge Meaning, Not Formatting**: Focus on whether the academic content, scope, and level of the two statements fit together. Minor structural issues (such as numbering styles or formatting) do not constitute academic misalignment unless they compromise meaning.
4. **Context Matters**: Always read the surrounding weekly context in the Case Booklet. A relationship is not judged in a vacuum; the week's lesson context illuminates what the statements intend to achieve.
5. **Beyond Bloom's Verbs Alone**: For **ILO $\leftrightarrow$ CLO** mappings, do not simply match action verbs. Evaluate whether the ILO genuinely develops the competency and developmental stature expected by the CLO.

## 3. THE FOUR DISCRETE ACADEMIC JUDGMENTS

In Column E of your workbook, select exactly one of the four institutional judgments:

| Code | Judgment | When to Choose |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **`ALIGNED`** | **Aligned** | The evidence fully supports the relationship. Content, scope, and level are mutually coherent with no material gap or pedagogical conflict. |
| **`PARTIALLY_ALIGNED`** | **Partially Aligned** | There is meaningful alignment, but a material gap, ambiguity, or scope mismatch remains that would affect instructional delivery or assessment. |
| **`NOT_ALIGNED`** | **Not Aligned** | There is a substantiated contradiction or disconnect. The two statements concern unrelated subject matter or incompatible objectives. |
| **`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`** | **Insufficient Evidence** | The supplied text cannot support a defensible judgment because statements are excessively brief, circular, or worded in purely generic terms (e.g., "Lecture using PowerPoint", "Quiz on topics discussed"). |

## 4. STEP-BY-STEP WORKBOOK COMPLETION GUIDE

Open your Excel workbook (`faculty-evaluation-form-[SubjectCode].xlsx`). Data cells are locked to protect against accidental edits; you will type only in the **yellow cells**.

```
+------------+-----------------------+---------------------------+-----------------------------------+
| Case ID    | Faculty Judgment      | Academic Justification    | Recommended Correction            |
| (Col A)    | (Col E - Dropdown)    | (Col F - Mandatory Text)  | (Col G - Conditional Text)        |
+------------+-----------------------+---------------------------+-----------------------------------+
| FV-01      | ALIGNED               | Exact quotes + rationale  | None                              |
| FV-02      | PARTIALLY_ALIGNED     | Exact quotes + gap named  | Proposed constructive revision    |
+------------+-----------------------+---------------------------+-----------------------------------+
```

### Step 1: Select Your Judgment (Column E)
Click the cell in Column E to open the dropdown menu, and select `ALIGNED`, `PARTIALLY_ALIGNED`, `NOT_ALIGNED`, or `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

### Step 2: Write Your Academic Justification (Column F) — *Mandatory*
Write 1 to 3 clear sentences explaining why you selected that judgment.
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Mandatory Quotation Rule**: You **must** quote exact words or key phrases from both compared statements within quotation marks (e.g., *While the content specifies "Java methods and syntax", the ILO requires students to "implement basic algorithms" without addressing...*).

### Step 3: Propose a Recommended Correction (Column G)
- **If you chose `PARTIALLY_ALIGNED` or `NOT_ALIGNED`**: You **must** provide a recommended correction. Name the field that should be improved (e.g., Topic description, ILO wording, activity design, or assessment task) and suggest constructive wording.
- **If you chose `ALIGNED`**: Type `"None"`.
- **If you chose `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`**: Propose what specific syllabus details should be added to make the entry evaluable.

### Step 4: Record Reviewer Metadata (Columns H, I, J)
- **Reviewer ID (Column H)**: Enter your assigned reviewer code (e.g., `R-IT-01`). Do **not** enter your personal name.
- **Within Expertise? (Column I - Optional)**: Select `"Yes"`, `"No"`, or `"Uncertain"`.
- **Requires Institutional Decision? (Column J - Optional)**: Select `"Yes"` if your judgment depends on an undefined institutional policy (e.g., how values-formation ILOs should relate to technical engineering topics).
- **Notes (Column K - Optional)**: Note any typographical issues, structural formatting concerns, or general observations.

## 5. CLO CLASSIFICATION REFERENCE

For ILO $\leftrightarrow$ CLO cases, remember the LCUP developmental progression:
- **CLO 1 (`Introductory`)**: Establishes foundational concepts, definitions, and baseline principles.
- **CLO 2 (`Enabling`)**: Develops intermediate analytical and procedural skills; guided application.
- **CLO 3 (`Demonstrating`)**: Synthesizes autonomous mastery, advanced implementation, and practical execution.
- **CLO 4 (`Integration of Values`)**: Integrates Marian Augustinian values, ethics, and social responsibility into technical practice.

Assess whether the weekly ILO is calibrated to the appropriate developmental depth of the mapped CLO.

3. Return the completed Excel workbook file directly to the Research Coordinator via institutional email or secure local campus exchange.
4. Keep your completed file confidential until the formal adjudication process has concluded.
