System overview

The AI-Powered Web-Based Syllabus Management System is one platform for preparing, checking, reviewing, approving, and storing course syllabi. It was developed for the College of Information Technology and Engineering (CITE) of La Consolacion University Philippines.

It answers a common problem: syllabi kept in separate files, checked by eye, and reviewed through scattered messages, with no single record of the review.

Objectives

To standardize how course syllabi are prepared, checked, reviewed, approved, and stored.

  • Produce syllabi that follow LCUP's template through a builder, PDF import, and AI drafting.
  • Check compliance and constructive alignment, and suggest revisions.
  • Route each syllabus through an ordered review before the VPAA decides.
  • Monitor deadlines, submission status, and program compliance.
  • Keep approved syllabi in a secure repository with version history and an audit log.

Target users

The faculty, academic officers, and students of the college department.

Faculty prepare, check, submit, and track their syllabi.
Administrator (Dean) reviews first and manages users, curriculum, and deadlines.
Asst. VPAA and LIC Director review next, in order.
VPAA makes the final decision on every syllabus.
Students view the approved syllabi of their subjects.

Key features and functionalities

1. Prepare

The seven-step Syllabus Builder fills in official course data and saves drafts automatically. Faculty can also import a PDF or start from a draft made by the AI Syllabus Generator.

2. Check

Coded rules stop a syllabus with missing sections or a grading total other than 100%. The AI-assisted Checker reviews alignment from graduate attributes to weekly tasks, gives a score, and suggests revisions to accept, edit, or reject. AI advises; people decide.

3. Review and approve

The Dean, Asst. VPAA, and LIC Director clear their stages in order. Only the VPAA approves. A returned syllabus goes back to the reviewer who returned it.

4. Store and monitor

Approved syllabi stay in a searchable repository with version history. Dashboards, analytics, and CSV or DOCX reports show progress.

Also built in: deadlines and reminders, messaging, notifications, audit log, role-based access, COR-verified student registration, English and Filipino, desktop and mobile.
