01 — System overview

One guided process instead of scattered files and manual checking

The system lets the College of Information Technology and Engineering (CITE) of La Consolacion University Philippines prepare, check, review, approve, and store its course syllabi on one platform.

02 — Objectives

To standardize how course syllabi are prepared, checked, reviewed, approved, and stored.

  1. 1Produce template-based syllabi by guided form, PDF import, or AI drafting.
  2. 2Check each syllabus against coded rules and constructive alignment.
  3. 3Guide each syllabus through an ordered review before the VPAA decides.
  4. 4Monitor progress with dashboards, deadlines, reports, and an audit log.
