LCUP Core Values.


Compassion
“What does love look like? It has the hands to help others. It has the feet to hasten to the poor and needy. It has eyes to see misery and want. It has the ears to hear the sighs and sorrows of men. That is what love looks like.” — St. Augustine, Confessions
Compassion is love in action. It means going beyond sympathy and extending genuine care by understanding the feelings of others, recognizing their needs, and responding with kindness. Through compassion, we reflect God’s love by becoming instruments of comfort and support to those who are most in need.


Courage
“Right is right, even if no one is doing it; wrong is wrong, even if everyone is doing it.” — St. Augustine
Courage is the strength to stand firm in truth and goodness. It is the freedom to express one’s thoughts responsibly, the boldness to give and receive fraternal correction, and the integrity to choose what is right even when it is difficult or unpopular. Rooted in Gospel values, courage empowers us to live authentically and faithfully in service of God and others.


Community-orientedness
“In essentials, unity; in doubtful things, liberty; in all things, charity.” — St. Augustine
We believe in the power of unity. By fostering friendship, mutual trust, and respect, we build a community that is interdependent and socially responsive. This value challenges each of us to go beyond personal interests, working together with dedication and shared responsibility to achieve common goals that uplift both the University and society.


Humility
"Do you wish to rise? Begin by descending. You plan a tower that will pierce the cloudes? Lay first the foundation of humility." -St.Augustine, Sermon 69
Humility allows us to see ourselves and others through the lens of truth and grace. It teaches us to acknowledge both our strengths and limitations, to welcome correction with openness, and to use every experience as an opportunity for growth. Above all, humility reminds us to be grateful to God who created each of us uniquely for a purpose greater than ourselves.


Interiority
“Do not go outside yourself, return within. In the inward man dwells the truth.” — St. Augustine, De Vera Religione
Interiority is the practice of turning inward, encountering our true selves, and allowing God’s presence to transform us. Through prayer, reflection, and contemplative silence, we confront our weaknesses and sinfulness, but also rediscover our dignity as children of God. This journey within leads us to an authentic relationship with Him and guides us to live with clarity and peace.


Missionary Spirit
“The degree to which you are concerned for the common good rather than for your own, is the criterion by which you can judge how much progress you have made.” — St. Augustine, City of God
A missionary spirit calls us to live beyond ourselves, reaching out to others with faith, hope, and love. It is the boldness to respond to the needs of the times, the willingness to witness Christ through our words and actions, and the passion to work for the common good. This spirit drives us to be agents of transformation in the world.
